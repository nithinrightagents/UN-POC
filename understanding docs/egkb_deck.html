Where OSI sits in the bigger picture: The E-Government Development Index (EGDI) is a composite of three equal parts — Online Service Index (OSI, produced here), Telecommunication Infrastructure Index (TII, sourced from ITU), and Human Capital Index (HCI, sourced from UNESCO). This platform handles OSI only. MSQ data informs assessors as context; it never influences a country's ranking.

OSI and LOSI are separate workflows — different indicator sets, units of study, and scope — but they run on the same platform, share the same assessment engine, and write to the same database. National OSI covers 193 member states; LOSI covers city portals with custom instances (e.g., LOSI UK).

Where AI comes in 04 / 06

Inside the pre-fill step

Find

Link resolution — prior-cycle knowledge base, then the MSQ's declared URLs, then web search.

Look

Live traversal — the real portal is fetched and rendered; screenshots and snippets are stored as evidence.

Judge

Two independent agents — Gemini on Vertex AI, one literal reader and one inferential, no shared context.

Check

Validator — each agent's claim is re-verified against the page it cited before it counts.

Settle

Adjudicator — agreement becomes a suggestion with a confidence score. Results below 75% confidence go directly to the human escalation queue and are never surfaced as a pre-fill suggestion.

The output is a suggestion with evidence attached. Two humans still answer independently, and their answers are what score the country.

Honest boundaries 06 / 06

What this prototype is not, and what comes next

Out of scope today

  • Authentication — role is a form choice, not a login. First thing a production build adds.
  • Drupal / UN hosting — no CMS integration attempted.
  • TII and HCI — external ITU and UNESCO feeds untouched.
  • Semantic MSQ matching — structural parse only, not answer-to-indicator mapping.

Next

  • Benchmark the AI against published 2024 OSI scores to quantify pre-fill accuracy per module.
  • Roles and access for admin, assessor, reviewer, senior reviewer.
  • Publication target aligned to UN infrastructure and design specs.
  • Migrate 4–5 prior cycles of spreadsheet history into the knowledge base.

Reminder of the constraint that shapes all of it: consultant-led delivery, prepaid long-term hosting, and no autonomous AI scoring — ever.
